# Prompt Asset Statis Postly

Sembilan asset D02 dibuat dengan image generation tool lalu dinormalisasi secara
lokal menjadi WebP. Seluruh tokoh bersifat fiktif, tidak mempunyai dependency
runtime eksternal, dan hanya dipakai untuk instructor demo Postly.

## Gambar Post

Semua gambar post menggunakan gaya `photorealistic-natural`, komposisi landscape
untuk social feed, tanpa teks, logo, watermark, atau UI frame. Focal point dibuat
aman untuk center crop.

### `post-coffee.webp`

> A warm cup of latte with elegant leaf latte art on a simple wooden café
> table; quiet contemporary café with a softly blurred plant; single off-white
> ceramic cup; soft warm morning window light; realistic ceramic, foam, and
> wood grain.

### `post-workspace.webp`

> A clean developer workspace with an open slim laptop, a small green plant,
> notebook, and pen; bright minimal home office; slightly elevated
> three-quarter view; dark laptop screen without readable text; soft neutral
> daylight.

### `post-city.webp`

> A modern Southeast Asian city skyline from an elevated rooftop near sunset;
> dense contemporary city with a pale blue-to-peach sky; wide view with a clear
> horizon; soft golden-hour light and realistic atmospheric haze.

### `post-code.webp`

> A close view of a laptop displaying colorful generic source code in a dark
> editor; dim modern developer desk with warm lamp bokeh; angled close-up;
> balanced blue screen light and warm ambient light; no credentials or product
> names.

### `post-mountain.webp`

> A tranquil alpine mountain lake surrounded by evergreen trees and rugged
> peaks; wide centered mountain and reflection; early golden-hour sunlight;
> detailed rock, pine trees, still water, and small traces of snow.

## Avatar Seed

head-and-shoulders, eye-level, aman untuk circular crop, background biru-abu
lembut, cahaya natural, tanpa teks, logo, aksesori, watermark, atau UI frame.
